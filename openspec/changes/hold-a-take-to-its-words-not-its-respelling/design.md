## Context

Read in the engine at the lane's head; the files named here are byte for byte those of `v0.26.1`, which ran the trials [S1].

- **A take is held to its respelling.** `judgment.measured` compares a take's transcript with the thought's spoken text and with nothing else (`judgment.py:298-299`). The spoken text is the display text after the contract's normalization (`pronunciation.py:119-127`): a `homophone` rule writes another character of the same sound where the narrator misreads the written one (`去重` as `去虫`), and an `english-name` rule writes a name as the narrator should say it (`ETHOS` as `Eethos`). The `units` line allows no unit to differ by reading (`UNIT_LIMIT = 0`, `judgment.py:96`, `judgment.py:406`).
- **A recognizer writes the word, not the respelling.** The pinned recognizer wrote `eethos` in none of 320 recorded occurrences. In the English edition it wrote `ethos` in all 93. It wrote `就当` and `去重` in all 30 takes of three Chinese thoughts that say them (02.4, 04.3, 04.5). It also writes the respelling: a fourth thought, 07.1, says `去重` twice, its bound take's transcript holds `去虫` both times, and it passes today, the characters being the same. [S2] [S11]
- **By reading, those still differ.** A Latin unit's reading is its letters (`recognition.py:107-108`, `recognition.py:123-124`): `eethos` is not `ethos`. A Han character's reading is the dictionary's in context unless a declared word covers it (`recognition.py:110-111`): the dictionary reads `重` in `去重` as `zhòng` and `当` in `就当` as `dāng`, the very readings the film respells against. A film cannot declare the display word's reading: the readings check reads the spoken text and refuses a declared `去重` as never said (`readings.py:235-239`, `readings.py:263-269`). [S2]
- **In Chinese speech the recognizer has no spelling for the name.** Over 197 Chinese takes it wrote the name's 227 occurrences as `ethos` 74 times and in nine other ways 153 times (`isos` 75, `esos` 57, `ezos` 6, `iso` 4, the Han word `伊索` 4, `ethers` 3, `esox` 2, `esoes` 1, `eidos` 1). The released Chinese edition's own recordings are written `ethos` 10 times, `Eso s` 6, `isos` 5 and `e source` once. Every Chinese transcript that holds the name more than once writes it one way throughout: the ten takes of 03.7, which says it four times (`EsoS` at all 40 places), and the six such segments of the released edition. That reads as the recognizer choosing a spelling for a transcript, not as a hearing of each occurrence (an inference). [S2] [S3]
- **The trials.** The Chinese ETHOS edition ended two rounds and the run-on round with 23 of 57 thoughts unsettled after 216 candidates, 1,740 s; the English edition, with no round and the run-on round, with 24 of 57 after 84 candidates, 1,228 s. Every unsettled Chinese thought stands at a respelling: 20 say the name, 3 say a respelled Han word. Of the English ones, 20 are the name alone and 4 hold an English word heard as another. [S4]
- **The owner's ear, for four takes.** On 22 November at 13:20 the owner heard four Chinese takes of the trial, written `Ethos` (01.6, 09.2), `Isos` (03.1) and `ESOs` (03.8), and answered that the name is read correctly (owner-words D-20). That a recognizer has no steady spelling for a foreign name in Chinese speech probably carries over (an inference); the counts do not. [S11]
- **The term line exists and no film uses it.** A film may declare a Latin term in `[[narration.terms]]`; the judgment then compares the first vowel of each occurrence with that of a reference occurrence (`terms.py:435-460`), and the readings check holds every language to the declared spoken form (`terms.py:100-123`). That a person heard it is the film's word; no code reads it. [S5]
- **Two archived decisions are reopened by their own conditions.** The comparison by reading: "Reopened when: a film's transcript shows a difference by reading that its owner hears as none". The limit of zero, the owner's decision of 18 November: "Reopened when: a film's first takes are refused for units in more than three thoughts of 46". ## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**

- A take that says its text is no longer refused by the `units` line for a word that the film's contract respells, a Han word or a Latin name. What this delivers on the recorded takes, edition by edition, stands in the Migration Plan: it does not by itself let every edition converge (decision 6).
- The `units` line still refuses a take that drops a word or says another in its place.
- A film that respells a Latin name is told what it must declare before any speech model loads, by whichever command it runs first: a narration, `judge`, `converge` or `audition`.
- No take that is accepted today is refused, in any film.

**Non-Goals:**

- A change of `UNIT_LIMIT`, or an exception for a thought.
- Words that a recognizer hears as another where the contract respells nothing (`kernel` and `colonel`, `and` and `in`, `记入` and `记录`): decision 6 sends them to a Change of their own.
- A measure of how a name is said beyond its first vowel.
- A binding of a term's reference to the take a person heard.
- A change of `converge`, of `adoption`, or of a film's declaration form.
- The two counts that compare a segment with its display text by character, the transcription's `unit_error_rate` and the review's `differing_units` (`transcription.py:128-129`, `review/measurement.py:422-442`): they stay as they are.

## Decisions

Each decision states what it rests on, what was considered, why the option taken was chosen, where it applies and what would reopen it.

1. **The seam: a take is held to its words, and a respelling is how the narrator is told a word, not the word.**
   - **Rests on:** the first three items of the Context. The `units` line asks whether the words of the thought were said. A respelling is written for the narrator; a recognizer answers in the word's own spelling. Comparing the two refuses takes that say their text.
   - **Considered:** (a) the comparison as it is, with the film's owner changing the text, which no change of text settles, since the text is right; (b) comparing with the display text in place of the spoken text; (c) giving the transcript the contract's normalization as a string, as the text is given it; (d) keeping the spoken text as the basis and reading a respelled word as one word under both spellings, by the two owners that already read words: the declared words for Han characters (decision 2) and the declared term for a Latin name (decision 3).
   - **Chosen because:** (d). The spoken text stays the one basis of the alignment, the fingerprints and the comparison, and each respelling is read by the owner of its kind. For a name the seam runs between three questions, each with one owner: which written forms are the term is `terms.spoken_forms`' (`terms.py:90-97`); whether a unit stands in the transcript where the text holds the term is the `units` line's (`recognition.reading_distance`); how the term was said there is the term line's (`terms.take_terms`). The `units` line never judges a sound, and the term line never judges whether a word is present: it cannot, since the aligner is forced on the spoken text and places the term's word whether it was said or not (`judgment.py:288`). (b) turns the fault round: a transcript that holds the spoken form (`去虫`) would differ from the display text, and the alignment and the comparison would then rest on two texts. (c) is one function, but it rewrites the transcript: where the recognizer drops a mark between two characters that the rule's word joins (`成就，当然` written `成就当然`), the rewriting puts `荡` where the text holds `当`, and a take that says its text is refused; (d) reads `当` there by the rule and still finds the same character. [S7]
   - **Applies to:** `narration/recognition.py`, which owns the comparison; `readings.py` and `terms.py`, which own the two kinds of word; `judgment.bench`, which hands them to the comparison.
   - **Reopened when:** a third kind of normalization rule is added to `pronunciation._normalized`.
2. **A Han word that the contract respells reads as its spoken form reads, in a text and in a transcript.**
   - **Rests on:** the three Chinese thoughts 02.4, 04.3 and 04.5, refused in 10 of 10 takes each for `当` and `重` read by the dictionary; `行情`, respelled `航情`, which passes only because the dictionary happens to agree; the essentials film's `调用`, respelled `掉用`, which passes for the same reason. [S2]
   - **Considered:** (a) the film declares the display word in its contract with a recorded disagreement: refused today as a word the narration never says, and a second copy of what the rule already states; (b) the rule's own `reading` field, which the films' contracts write and no code reads or checks (`pronunciation._normalized` reads `display`, `spoken` and `kind`, `pronunciation.py:130-149`); (c) each `homophone` rule gives the comparison a word, the rule's display word read as the dictionary reads the rule's spoken form, beside the contract's declared words.
   - **Chosen because:** (c) derives the reading from the spoken form, which is what the narrator is given, so the two cannot disagree as an unchecked field can, and it uses the path a declared word already takes (`readings.coverage`, `recognition.read_units`). Two characters that are the same stay alike whatever their readings (`recognition.py:126`). The word adds likeness where the film itself says the two are one word, and it removes likeness in one case, which is decided here and tested: a transcript that writes the display word where the text holds another character with the reading the film respells against (text `去众`, transcript `去重`: alike today, one unit apart after). No recorded take does this (Risks). [S11]
   - **Applies to:** `readings.py`: one function that gives the words the comparison reads by, the declared words and each `homophone` rule's word, so that `judgment.bench` asks one owner and joins nothing itself. It cannot live in `declared_words`: `resolve` would refuse the rule's word as never said. [S11]
   - **As built:** `readings.compared_words(contract)`: the declared words, then a word for each `homophone` rule whose display and spoken forms are both Han, its syllables `contextual(spoken)`; `judgment.bench` asks it in place of `declared_words`. A `homophone` rule on Latin letters gives no word, as the note's decision 5 states. [S12]
   - **Reopened when:** a film respells a Han word with a character that the dictionary reads in more than one way in its sentence; the reading of the spoken form alone may then differ from its reading in the text, and the take is refused, not wrongly accepted.
3. **A declared term is one unit under its display form and its spoken form.**
   - **Rests on:** the English edition: 93 of 93 occurrences written `ethos` or `ethos's`, each refused today against `eethos`. `terms.says` already holds what says a term: the term or its spoken form, in any case, a possessive aside (`terms.py:90-97`).
   - **Considered:** (a) every `english-name` rule read this way, declared or not; (b) only a term the film declares, by `terms.spoken_forms`.
   - **Chosen because:** (b). Who says a term has one owner, and decision 5 makes a respelled name a declared term before any take exists, so (a) would read the same names by a second rule. A name that is not declared keeps today's comparison, which refuses it.
   - **Applies to:** `recognition.read_units`: a Latin unit that says a declared term reads as that term, its possessive kept, on both sides. In English nothing else changes: a name written in other letters still differs by one unit.
   - **One owner of the possessive:** `terms.says` strips it (`terms.py:95-97`) and the comparison must keep it (a possessive against a bare form differs). One function in `terms.py` says which form a unit says and whether it is possessive; `says` and `read_units` both use it, and `recognition.py` holds no second pattern. [S11]
   - **What the comparison is handed:** today the text, the transcript and the words. Decisions 3 and 4 add the edition's terms, its other Latin units and whether it is the Chinese edition. `judgment.bench` builds one value from their owners once and hands it whole; `words` stays the third positional argument that the existing tests pass. [S11]
   - **Reopened when:** a film needs a name respelled as several words of other letters (a term's spoken form is one Latin word, `film.py:374-376`); no film has one.
   - **As built:** `terms.said_form(word, forms)` gives the casefolded form a word says and whether it says that form's possessive, and `terms.says` asks it. `recognition.Read` gains `term`; `read_units(text, words, declared)` reads a Latin unit that says a declared term as the letters of the term's spoken form, `s` added for a possessive, so a curly apostrophe in the text, which parts its units, is joined again by `_joined`. The value handed to the comparison is `recognition.Names` (the terms; decision 4 adds the rest), built once by `judgment.bench` and passed as `reading_distance(expected, observed, words, names)`. After review 1 (S4), `alike` also holds two Latin units alike whose own letters and digits are the same, so a term declared beside `names` does not part it from a transcript's `name's`, which says the term's possessive.    - **Applies to:** nothing here. To that Change go the two reopened decisions of the Context, the recognizer told the film's names (option (e) of decision 4) and the help of `--rounds 0`.
   - **Reopened when:** not applicable.
7. **A first narration from a new voice needs no new command; the reference is heard after the first run.**
   - **Rests on:** the last item but one of the Context.
   - **Considered:** (a) the order as the commands give it; (b) a state "reference not yet heard" in which the term line is recorded and not judged; (c) the declaration binding the digest of the take a person heard, refused when another take stands in its place.
   - **Chosen because:** (a) for this Change. The order is:
     1. The film declares the term with a reference occurrence in each edition: a thought and an index, which the text gives before any take exists.
     2. `converge` narrates and judges. The reference thought's first take is the standard; a take of that thought passes the term line where it shows two formants (`terms.py:440-458`).

     4. (c) would close this, at the cost of a tracked edit at every retake of the reference thought; it is a decision for the owner and a Change of its own.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- **[A name said with another consonant or ending passes in Chinese]** → The term line holds the first vowel; a person hears the reference; the review's listening plan lists every occurrence with its vowel. Before this Change the `units` line refused such a take only when the recognizer happened to write other letters, and with it takes of the kind the released edition is made of: 12 of that edition's 22 occurrences are written in other letters.
- **[What the term line says of today's takes is not known]** → No film has run with a term, and neither this note nor its review read audio. It is measured before the build (Migration Plan, step 1): decision 5 takes away the film's way to narrate without the term line, and decision 4 hands the name's sound to it. One sensitivity is inferred from the code and not measured: a resonance counts as a formant only above 200 Hz (`terms.py:70`), and a close vowel that loses its first formant to that floor would be misread; the Chinese voice has changed. [S11]
- **[A reference that nobody has heard]** → Decision 7 states the order and what it leaves open.
- **[A declared term gives up its letters in Chinese speech]** → For a name that the recognizer spells steadily (`Agent`, `GitLab`), declaring it as a term buys the vowel check and gives up the letters. No rule asks a film to declare such a name; decision 5 asks it only of a respelled one.
- **[One take accepted today could be refused]** → Only where a transcript writes a respelled word (`去重`) at a place where the text holds another character whose dictionary reading is the one the film respells against (`zhòng`). No such take stands among the 1,109 recorded takes of the six editions compared, the full film's Chinese edition included. [S8] [S11]
- **[BREAKING for the ETHOS film]** → The readings check names the name, its spoken form and the thoughts; the declaration is five lines; the release says so.
